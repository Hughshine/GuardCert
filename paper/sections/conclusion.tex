\section{Conclusion and Remaining Work}

GuardCert's implemented kernel composes executable-check and transformation
certificates without fixing a source language or optimizer. Its CompCert instance
makes check safety, private-state transport, and installation concrete for
supported loop transformations. The nested loaded-bound proof path derives
the cached model from checks licensed by original-source execution and connects
it to the existing candidate and whole-program proofs. The extracted frontend
compiler installs interchange and tiling on the fixed-layout adapted source,
with observed candidate and fallback behavior in assembly. Wider source/context
coverage, affordable sufficient conditions, and measurements of reuse and cost
remain necessary for the final case-study and contribution claims.
